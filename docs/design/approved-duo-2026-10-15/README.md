## Implementation notes
These are approved visual approaches, not screenshots of a shipping implementation or proof of native fit. Recipe text, timings, yield values, hardware and system chrome in generated concepts are illustrative. Production content comes from saved recipes and the timing engine.
Both recipes belong to one coordinated dinner. Reading, switching tabs, selecting a recipe and rotating must preserve reading positions, cooking state and timer deadlines. Actions follow existing engine eligibility and completion checks. Cooking remains cream; the leaf is decorative and must not crowd controls. Validate real safe areas/system rail, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and reachability before release.
Three-/four-dish Cooking adaptations are not approved by inference from these images. Earlier drafts and superseded layouts are deliberately excluded.
