Privacy Policy

Dinner, Together for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 10, 2026

Dinner, Together does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits in the app.

What the app keeps, and where

The app saves one thing: the dinner you are planning (the menu you picked, your chosen dinner time, which steps you have started or finished, and the timing notes it shows you). That file lives only inside the app's own storage on your device. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules timer reminders on your device using Apple's local notification system. They are created and delivered entirely on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Notifications → Dinner, Together, and the app's timers keep working without them.

No tracking

The app contains no tracking of any kind and makes no network connections. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — the change will be described here, with the date above updated, before that version is released.
