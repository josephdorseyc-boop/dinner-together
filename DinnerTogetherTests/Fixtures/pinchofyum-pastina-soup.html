Oh, This Is Good.

Like, slurp that savory broth and go back for a third bowl kind of good.

My daughter got sick last week, which means that our whole family got sick last week. And when I get sick, I lose two things: 1) my appetite, and 2) my interest in cooking.

This pastina soup truly SAVED us last week. It is:

  • Incredibly easy to make.
  • So, so savory and flavorful.
  • Light (like, practically drinkable) but nutrient-rich.
  • Warm, cozy, and healing.
  • Generous – like, every time I make it, I end up freezing half of the broth for our next sick day, or to share with someone when they might need it.

I can’t say enough about how much I loved this last week. I sat down at the table with a steamy bowl with extra parm on top, and found myself repeatedly going back for “just one more scoop” of the broth over those tiny stars, even though I swore I hadn’t been feeling hungry just a moment earlier.

My girls were pretty excited about eating tiny star pasta with olive oil + Parmesan, while Bjork and I ladled the luscious broth over our bowls and finally tucked into some nurturing food for the first time in several days.

Pastina refers to the tiny star shaped pasta, which I found at Target, and the nickname Italian Penicillin refers to the healing nature of the savory broth – almost like a chicken noodle soup, but simpler. (And if I’m being honest, I prefer this to chicken noodle.)

I hope you are healthy and well, physically and emotionally. But if you’re not, this is comfort meal that has been serving our family so well lately!

Source Notes: “Italian penicillin” is a nickname for pastina soup, or pastina (small pasta) in broth. It is playfully compared to medicine because of its healing vibe, being that it is a warm, nostalgic, nourishing comfort food. It originated with generations of Italian grandmothers and it has moved into the spotlight after becoming a popular trend on TikTok over the last few years. 🙂

Description

This soup has become my go-to recipe when my family is sick. The broth is amazing – simple, light, flavorful, and nourishing! It’s absolute comfort food. 

Ingredients

Units Scale

For the Broth:

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 large carrots, peeled and cut into large chunks
  • 2 stalks of celery, cut into large chunks
  • half of 1 yellow onion, peeled and cut into large chunks
  • 4 cloves garlic, peeled and smashed
  • 10 cups chicken or vegetable broth (I use 10 cups water plus 2 tablespoons Better Than Bouillon chicken base)
  • 1 teaspoon salt

For Serving: 

  • 1 1/2 cups pastina (I use Stelline from the Good and Gather brand at Target)
  • freshly grated Parmesan cheese for topping

Instructions

  1. Sauté Veggies: Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the chunky carrots, celery, onion, and garlic to the pot. Sauté for 5 minutes. 
  2. Simmer: Add broth and salt. Simmer for 30 minutes, until the vegetables are very tender.
  3. Remove Veggies: Use a slotted spoon to transfer the vegetable chunks to a bowl. Set aside to cool for a few minutes (you don’t want to add scorching hot veggies to your plastic blender). 
  4. Blend: When the veggies have cooled a bit, blend them with 1-2 cups of broth until very smooth. Add the blended mixture back to the pot; stir to incorporate.
  5. Pastina: Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain and toss with olive oil and a pinch of salt. 
  6. Serve: Put a scoop of pastina in a shallow bowl. Ladle the steaming-hot broth over the top, then top each bowl with freshly grated Parmesan. 
  • Prep Time: 5 minutes
  • Cook Time: 40 minutes
  • Category: Soup
  • Method: Stovetop
  • Cuisine: Italian
